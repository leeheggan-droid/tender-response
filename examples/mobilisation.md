## Longer narrative

### Mobilisation

We propose building readiness in stages so your team can judge when the service
is ready to transfer. The 20-working-day approach begins once the agreed asset
register and access permissions are available. Days 1–5 validate assets and risks;
days 6–15 transfer work orders and train staff; days 16–20 test readiness and
agree handover. This sequence brings preparation and testing together before
the handover decision, supporting your objective of an orderly transition. Staffing
allocation and the start date remain subject to confirmation.

### Governance and escalation

A shared issue register gives your team a clear view of open issues, their owners
and the actions being taken. Weekly progress reviews provide a regular opportunity
to decide what needs attention before handover. The Transition Director is
accountable; unresolved issues escalate to the Operations Director within one
working day. These proposed controls connect visible responsibilities to your
need for timely readiness decisions.

### Evidence of comparable delivery

An approved August 2026 closeout records a comparable Hong Kong managed workplace-services
transition at one office. It completed in 20 working days following receipt of
complete asset data and access. The client signed the readiness checklist before
handover, and no priority-one service incidents were recorded during transition.
Documented acceptance before handover makes this relevant to your readiness
objective in a similar service and location. It does
not establish the absence of every incident type or guarantee the same result
for a different scope.
