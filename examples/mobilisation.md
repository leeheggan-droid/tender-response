## Longer narrative

### Mobilisation

We propose a 20-working-day transition, beginning once the agreed asset register
and access permissions are available. Days 1–5 validate assets and risks;
days 6–15 transfer work orders and train staff; days 16–20 test readiness and
agree handover. For a buyer prioritising uninterrupted service, this sequence
provides an explicit readiness check before responsibility transfers. Staffing
allocation and the start date remain subject to confirmation.

### Governance and escalation

The Transition Director is accountable for the proposed approach. A shared issue
register records owners, actions and closure, with client progress reviews each
week. Unresolved issues escalate to the Operations Director within one working
day. These controls make outstanding actions visible during transition and give
the buyer a defined escalation route.

### Evidence of comparable delivery

An approved closeout records a comparable Hong Kong managed workplace-services
transition at one office. It completed in 20 working days following receipt of
complete asset data and access. The client signed the readiness checklist before
handover, and no priority-one service incidents were recorded during transition.
This supports the proposed sequence in a similar service and location. It does
not establish the absence of every incident type or guarantee the same result
for a different scope.
