# Learn from supplied tender feedback

Use when the user supplies reviewer comments, scoring or a bid debrief. Keep the
record in the private tender workspace. Do not contact the buyer or modify this
public skill automatically.

1. Map each substantive comment to the exact question and published criterion.
   Record the comment, score if supplied, source/date and affected answer passage.
   Separate an evaluator's finding from your interpretation; a lost bid alone
   does not prove a writing defect.
2. Identify the actual cause: missed requirement, weak buyer relevance, insufficient
   evidence, unsupported claim, unclear method or delivery risk, format/limit error,
   or a commercial issue beyond writing. Prioritise mandatory failures, then the
   published scoring impact. Do not invent an improvement score or win probability.
3. For an evidence issue, actively re-search the permitted company sources using
   [the search method](company-evidence.md). Inspect underlying records and update
   the existing ledger. For a writing issue, revise the affected passage using
   existing proof; retain required scope, qualifications and commitments.
4. Check the revised answer against the original question, evidence and exact
   limits. Record the before/after passage and concrete check. A second reviewer
   can assess the revised answer when available; identify self-review honestly.
5. Save a reusable correction in the private response library: criterion/context,
   lesson, evidence IDs, owner/approval where needed, and last-checked date. Reuse
   the principle in later bids, then revalidate facts and tailor the answer. One
   buyer's preference is not a universal rule; unresolved claims stay unresolved.
