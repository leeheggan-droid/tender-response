# Transition standard TS-04

Version 4, 2026-09-15. Approved by Operations Director for use in proposals.
Owner: Transition Director. Applies to managed workplace services in Hong Kong.

Standard transition is 20 working days from receipt of an agreed asset register
and client access permissions. Days 1–5 validate assets and risks; days 6–15
transfer work orders and train staff; days 16–20 test readiness and agree handover.
The Transition Director is accountable. Unresolved issues escalate to the
Operations Director within one working day. A shared issue register records
owners, actions and closure; the client reviews progress weekly.

This is an approved standard approach, not a confirmed resourcing allocation
or unconditional commitment for any particular tender.
